A BIRTHDAY LETTER

Dear Elgene,

Happy 22nd birthday, beautiful.

I wanted to give you something that did not feel like it came off a shelf. Something that could hold a little bit of you, a little bit of me, and a little bit of whatever this thing between us has become.

You have this way of being both soft and impossible to ignore. You can be playful one minute, completely composed the next, and somehow both versions still feel unmistakably like you.

I love that I get to see more than just the polished version. I get the silly faces, the unplanned photos, the random moments, the quiet ones and the moments where neither of us is trying to make anything look perfect. Those are usually the moments I end up valuing most.

Turning 22 does not mean you suddenly need to know exactly where life is going. I hope this year gives you room to grow without rushing you. I hope it gives you reasons to feel proud of yourself, people who pour into you properly, and moments that feel so good you wish you could pause them.

I hope you keep choosing yourself, protecting your peace and remembering that being loved should never require you to shrink.

And selfishly, I hope I get to be around for more of your laughter, more ridiculous pictures, more ordinary days that unexpectedly become memories, and more versions of you that I have not met yet.

You deserve to be celebrated today—not only because it is your birthday, but because there is only one Elgene Jamelia Januarie, and this world has had twenty-two years of her now.

Happy birthday, baby. Welcome to Chapter 22.

With love,
Lesetja

JUST BETWEEN US

Elgene,

I do not know what every chapter ahead of us will look like, and I do not want to pretend that love means everything will always be effortless.

What I do know is that you matter to me in a way that has made ordinary moments feel important. A photo in a mirror. A stupid face. Standing next to each other outside somewhere. Things that would mean nothing to anyone else somehow become things I want to keep.

I hope I never become so familiar with you that I forget to appreciate you. I hope I keep noticing your smile, your softness, your stubbornness, your beauty, your humour, and all the little pieces that make you Elgene.

And if one day you ever wonder what I saw in you, come back here.

I saw you. And I wanted more of you in my life.

Always yours,
Lesetja 🤎
